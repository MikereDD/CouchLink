# CouchLink TV Remote v1.2-dev.5 Polish Checklist

## Button feedback

- Confirm each enabled TV Remote button produces one light haptic pulse when Premium haptics is enabled.
- Confirm no haptic pulse is produced when Premium haptics is disabled.
- Confirm disabled controls do not produce haptic feedback.

## Power guard

- Turn the TV off with one Power tap.
- Rapidly tap Power twice and confirm CouchLink does not immediately toggle the TV twice.
- Wake the TV with one Power tap and confirm automatic reconnect still completes.

## Input feedback

- Open Input and select HDMI 3.
- Confirm the dialog closes immediately.
- Confirm the TV switches to the Fire TV Stick.
- Confirm CouchLink reports “Switched to HDMI 3.” after sending the app link.

## Regression

- Home, Back, Settings, D-pad, OK.
- Volume up/down and Mute.
- Channel up/down and Google Live.
- Rewind one minute, Play/Pause, Forward one minute.
- Rotation, background/foreground, Wi-Fi interruption, and reconnect.
