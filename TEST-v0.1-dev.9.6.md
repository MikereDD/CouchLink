Both platforms should be rebuilt for this release. Android contains the new layout and icon; Windows contains the Xbox launch action.

## Android layout

1. Confirm Steam is the full-width primary tile.
2. Confirm GOG Galaxy is the full-width secondary tile.
3. Confirm row one is Xbox, EA app, Ubisoft.
4. Confirm row two is Rockstar, Epic, Amazon.
5. Confirm the Xbox icon is crisp, centered, and optically consistent with the other premium icons.
6. Confirm Command Deck remains reachable above bottom navigation.

## Launcher behavior

1. Tap Steam and confirm Big Picture opens.
2. Tap GOG and confirm GOG Galaxy launches or focuses.
3. Tap Xbox and confirm the Xbox app launches.
4. Test EA, Ubisoft, Rockstar, Epic, and Amazon.
